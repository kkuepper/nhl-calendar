How Hurricanes games reach Canadian TV in 2026–27

The Carolina Hurricanes have no Canadian regional broadcaster, so in Canada their games are only on national channels: Sportsnet, Sportsnet+ (exclusive Monday nights), Prime Video (exclusive Wednesdays), and TVA Sports in French. Games without a national broadcast are usually available with Sportsnet+ Premium’s out-of-market package.
