How Leafs games reach Canadian TV in 2026–27

Most Maple Leafs regional games are on TSN4 (27 this season), with only 2 on Sportsnet Ontario. Regional games air only in Toronto, Southwestern & Northern Ontario and parts of Northwestern Ontario, and are blacked out in the rest of Canada.

Most other Leafs games are national on Sportsnet (Saturdays with TVA Sports in French), Sportsnet+ on Monday nights, and Prime Video on Wednesdays. CBC does not carry the NHL this season.

This season’s schedule lists Maple Leafs regional broadcasts on TSN4 (27) and Sportsnet Ontario (2).
