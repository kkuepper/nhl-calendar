How Sens games reach Canadian TV in 2026–27

The Senators are a split market in 2026–27. Their regional games are on TSN5 in English and RDS in French; when RDS is busy the French broadcast moves to RDS2 or RDS Info. Regional broadcasts are only available in Eastern Ontario, Québec & Atlantic Canada, a territory shared with the Canadiens, and are blacked out elsewhere in Canada.

Everything else is national: Sportsnet (often Sportsnet One or Sportsnet East) on Saturdays and other nights, Sportsnet+ on Monday nights, Prime Video with exclusive Wednesday games, and TVA Sports as the national French broadcaster. CBC does not carry the NHL this season.

This season’s schedule lists Senators regional broadcasts on TSN5 (50), RDS (26), RDS2 (16), and RDS Info (8).
