Add NHL games to your calendar, with the Canadian TV channel

Every team on this site has a calendar feed (an .ics / iCalendar subscription). Each game shows up with the Canadian channel that carries it — Sportsnet, TSN, RDS, TVA Sports, Prime Video, or Sportsnet+ — and the calendar updates itself when the NHL changes a time or a broadcaster.

1. Find your team’s feed

Open your team’s page from the team list and use the calendar buttons on that page. The plain feed lists every game with all its channels; pick your subscriptions on the team page first if you want a personalised feed that marks or leaves out games you can’t watch. Plain feeds look like this: https://kkuepper.github.io/nhl-calendar/calendars/OTT.ics

2. Google Calendar

  1. Open your team’s page on this site and press “Google Calendar”. Google opens with the feed filled in; confirm with “Add”.
  2. Or, in Google Calendar on the web: next to “Other calendars” press +, choose “From URL”, paste the feed URL, and press “Add calendar”.
  3. Google’s phone apps can’t add a calendar by URL, but once added on the web it shows on your phone too. Google refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours, sometimes only once a day.

3. Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

  1. On iPhone, iPad, or Mac, open your team’s page and press “Apple Calendar”. Calendar asks whether to subscribe; confirm.
  2. Or add it by hand. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Calendar (Settings → Apps → Calendar on newer iOS) → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the feed URL. Mac: in Calendar choose File → New Calendar Subscription and paste the feed URL.
  3. On a Mac, set “Auto-refresh” to every hour or every day so broadcaster changes come through.

4. Outlook

  1. On your team’s page, press “Outlook.com” for a personal Microsoft account, or “Outlook 365” for a work or school Microsoft 365 account. Outlook opens with the feed filled in; confirm to add it.
  2. Or, in Outlook on the web: open Calendar, choose “Add calendar” → “Subscribe from web”, paste the feed URL, give it a name, and press “Import”. The same subscribe-from-web links are https://outlook.live.com/calendar/0/addfromweb?url= (Outlook.com) and https://outlook.office.com/calendar/0/addfromweb?url= (Microsoft 365).
  3. Classic Outlook for Windows: in Calendar choose Home → Add Calendar → From Internet, paste the feed URL, and confirm.

Who carries the NHL on Canadian TV in 2026–27

  • Sportsnet is the main national English broadcaster, including Saturday nights, when TVA Sports has the French broadcast.
  • Monday Night Hockey is on Sportsnet+, Rogers’ streaming service.
  • Wednesday national games are exclusive to Prime Video.
  • TVA Sports is the national French-language broadcaster.
  • CBC does not carry NHL games in 2026–27, so no games are on free over-the-air TV.
  • TSN and RDS only carry regional games of Canadian teams. Regional broadcasts are blacked out outside the team’s region; Sportsnet also has regional Canucks, Flames, and Oilers games.

Regional channels by team this season

  • Calgary Flames: Sportsnet West
  • Edmonton Oilers: Sportsnet West
  • Montréal Canadiens: TSN2, RDS
  • Ottawa Senators: TSN5, RDS, RDS2, RDS Info
  • Toronto Maple Leafs: TSN4, Sportsnet Ontario
  • Vancouver Canucks: Sportsnet Pacific
  • Winnipeg Jets: TSN3
